# GLOBAL REBIRTH VIDEO — structure (same formula, tradition-relative)

> Sibling of the buddhist v1 structure (STRUCTURE-SEGMENTS.md). Every stop
> = the six fields below + owner-frame + guard + callback. Runtime: 30 min
> for the first global cut; full 13-stop pass may split (see roster).

## The six fields per stop
1. **WHAT** — the theory in one breath.
2. **WHAT'S GOING ON** — the mechanism, plain, 2–3 lines.
3. **THE EXACT MOMENT OF DEATH** — the tradition's own definition (the
   buddhist v1's proven hook — every tradition has one).
4. **LOGICAL ARGUMENT** — their proof, verbatim where possible.
5. **PRIMACY CLAIM** — why they say they're the true one.
6. **BEST OBJECTION** — fairness law, opponent's own words.

## Universal beats (adapted from v1)
- Owner frame (shared: hook can re-run with a global montage).
- Chained logic (death-definition → birth-definition → time → tour).
- Transfer animation per stop (VISUAL-SPEC pipeline; body-skins per
  SERIES-GLOBAL).
- Shared ontology = FIVE carrier families (persists-as-self / without-self /
  re-created / relation-persists / simultaneity).
- Guard per stop (owner's objection bank grows).
- Cliffs back to v1 ("Buddhism was the only one where nobody's home").
