## Working title
**Buddhist Cosmologies, Actually Explained** (family: "Actually…" series ·
extension of `rebirth`) — candidates: "Every Buddhism Has a Different
Universe" · "Are Buddhist Cosmologies Real?" · "The Three Readings"

## Thesis
Buddhism is not one cosmology — it's at least five (sutta-minimalist,
Abhidhamma-31, six-realm wheel, Pure Land, Tibetan bardo-map, plus the
mind-only/emptiness counter-readings). The five-family table in the global
rebirth film mapped traditions against each other; this film maps ONE
tradition against ITSELF. And the owner's crux — "are they all mental?" —
is not a modern imposition: the canon already holds three incompatible
readings (realist-kammic, mind-made, deconstructed), each banked below with
primary text. The verdict the sources force: they are REAL ENOUGH TO BE
KAMMICALLY PRODUCED and CONVENTIONAL ENOUGH TO BE EMPTY — "shown" by
realms the wise do not observe (Toh174).

### CH.2 — THE SUTTA MINIMALISM (the small map)
What the early suttas actually pin down: world = the six internal/external
sense fields and their contact-chain. [READ — SN 35.107 Lokasamudaya,
FoJin tid 1408, banked]: "Lokassa, bhikkhave, samudayañca atthaṅgamañca
desessāmi… Cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca uppajjati cakkhuviññāṇaṃ" — "I will
teach the origin and disappearance of the world: eye and forms and
eye-consciousness arise… contact → feeling → craving → grasping → becoming
→ birth → aging-and-death." The world's origin IS the origin of contact.
No Sumeru, no cosmography required for liberation. The Buddha as world-
minimalist: the map is optional, the chain is not.

### CH.3 — THE 31 PLANES (the big map)
Abhidhamma/Visuddhimagga expansion: kāma/rūpa/arūpa, 11+16+4 = 31, every
plane a psychophysical address produced by kamma. [ACQUIRE: Vism chapter
extracts from in-house PDF — BPS Ñāṇamoli trans.]. The engine paragraph:
[READ — AN 10.216 Saṁsappanīya, FoJin tid 3713, banked]: "Kammassakā,
bhikkhave, sattā kammadāyādā kammayonī kammabandhū kammapaṭisaraṇā" —
beings are owners, heirs, WOMB, kindred and refuge of their actions; action
distinguishes them as inferior and superior. The cosmology has no creator
because it has a different engine: your deeds are the parents.

### CH.4 — THE GOD WHO WAS MADE BY MIND (the trapdoor)
[READ — DN 1 Brahmajāla, FoJin urn sc/dn1.1, banked Pali]: the first being
in the empty brahma-world, "manomayo pītibhakkho" (mind-made, fed on
rapture), alone for an aeon, concludes: "ahamasmi brahmā mahābrahmā …
kattā nimmātā … pitā bhūtabhabyānaṃ" — "I am Brahmā, the maker, the
creator, father of all that are and are to be. These beings were made by
me." Then beings are reborn there — and THEY believe him. The punchline
[banked]: "Iti mama ca manopaṇidhi, ime ca sattā itthattaṃ āgatā" — "by
MY MIND-PREFERENCE these beings came." The creator-god's own origin story
is mind-made + survivor bias. Canon built the theodicy trap into its own
opening fable. [READ — MN 99 Subho, FoJin urn sc/mn99.1]: the Buddha tells
the brahmin the "path to Brahmā's company" — it's the brahmavihāras.
Cosmology as practice-address: the heaven is reachable by method.

### CH.5 — THE WHEEL: SIX REALMS (the famous map)
The popular map: deva/asura/human/animal/hungry-ghost/hell — as karmic
weather-system. [READ — DN 26 Cakkavatti, FoJin urn sc/dn26.1, banked:
the decline to a ten-year lifespan ("girls marriageable at five"), food
flavors vanishing, weapons turning to grass] — TIME-cosmology: the world
doesn't just have layers, it DETERIORATES on a schedule, then recovers.
Shared-worldview paragraph: every school keeps the wheel; they disagree on
where the hub is (Abhidhamma = tables, Tibetan = poisons, Pure Land =
exit-ramp). [in-house: tibetan-book-of-dead-reference.md six-bardo table ·
three-pure-land-sutras.pdf · Vism PDF].

### CH.6 — THE PURE LAND: COSMOLOGY AS PROMISE (the exit)
The Pure Land is the odd member: a geographically specific cosmology
(Amitābha's Sukhāvatī, 48 vows) that functions as ONE-STEP transport — the
only school where the cosmology IS the soteriology. Connects to v1's five
families: "re-created by promise" family gets a buddhist member. [ACQUIRE:
exact lines from the in-house three-sutras PDF — Larger Sutra vow lines].

### CH.7 — ARE THEY MENTAL? (the crux — owner's question)
Three readings, all primary, none invented:
- **Realist-kammic** (CH.3): realms are produced like everything else —
  dependently, kammically, without a maker. [AN 10.216 again]
- **Mind-made** (CH.4): Dhp 1–2 [READ — FoJin urn sc/dhp1-20.1, banked]:
  "Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā" — "Phenomena are preceded
  by mind, ruled by mind, MADE OF MIND." The most-quoted line in every
  psychology-of-religion anthology — sitting two books from the 31-plane
  manuals. [ANALAYO IN HOUSE: analayo-rebirth.txt line ~9818 context].
  Plus MN 9 / SN 35.107: even "the world" as the Buddha uses the word is
  the sense-field field. The strong mental reading has primary feet.
- **Deconstructed** (the high-canonical cut) [READ — Kangyur Toh 174,
  FoJin urn 84k/174.1, banked]: "The wise understand the desire realm, the
  form realm, and the formless realm to be utterly nonexistent realms …
  The absence of realms is indicated and articulated by using realms …
  the wise thus understand the three realms to be nonrealms." — realms are
  VOCABULARY with no inherent existence: "shown" the way you'd show
  emptiness with a finger. And [READ — Dhp 167 range, urn sc/dhp167-178.1]:
  "Look upon the world as a bubble or a mirage."
Then the synthesis the sources actually force: mental ≠ unreal-for-you
(kamma still lands — CH.3), real ≠ solid (Toh174 + Dhp bubble), so the
question "are they all mental?" answers YES in register (mind-made,
mind-riddled, empty-of-self) and NO in address (the deed-womb produces
births, not moods). [GUARD: against both New-Age flattening ("all just
psychology" — then AN 10.216's birth-language loses its meaning) and
naive literalism ("solid heavens" — then the Buddha's own minimalism and
Toh174's nonrealms are ignored).]

### CH.8 — THE SHARED WORLDVIEW (the table — v1's device, internal)
Buddhism-vs-buddhism five-family analogue (structure seed — expand into
table): (1) engine = kamma, never creator (all schools) · (2) address =
dependent-arising, not substance (Madhyamaka makes this explicit, Abhidhamma
implies it in tables) · (3) hub = the human realm (only plane that can
practice; both maps agree) · (4) time = deteriorating cycles (DN 26 +
kaliyuga-adjacent echoes) · (5) the exit disagreements: map-study vs
poison-work vs vow-transport vs seeing-through. What they share even when
they contradict: NOBODY IS IN CHARGE.
