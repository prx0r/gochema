## Thesis
Buddhism is not one cosmology — it's at least five (sutta-minimalist,
Abhidhamma-31, six-realm wheel, Pure Land, Tibetan bardo-map, plus the
mind-only/emptiness counter-readings). Not one tradition against other
traditions — ONE tradition against ITSELF. And the owner's crux — "are they
all mental?" — is not a modern imposition: the canon already holds three
incompatible readings (realist-kammic, mind-made, deconstructed), each
banked below with primary text. The verdict the sources force: they are
REAL ENOUGH TO BE KAMMICALLY PRODUCED and CONVENTIONAL ENOUGH TO BE EMPTY
— "shown" by realms the wise do not observe (Toh174). Standalone video —
no callbacks to any other film.

## Sections (seed)

### CH.1 — FRAME: FIVE UNIVERSES IN ONE RELIGION (0:00–1:30)
Cold-open = owner's locked line, first frame: "the buddhist realms are
states of awareness not physical maps of the solar system." Then the
hook: the six realms are Buddhism's most famous map — and its least
agreed-on. Theravāda counts 31 planes, the Sanskrit traditions 32 or 33,
Tibet draws three cosmologies at once (Abhidharma, Kālacakra, Dzogchen)
with a 15th-century Tibetan commentary chapter devoted to resolving their
contradictions. Same religion, same Buddha, different universes. [DIR:
the classic bhavachakra dissolving into five competing map renderings —
31-plane tower, sutta-dot, pure-land horizon, bardo sequence, blank.]

### CH.2 — THE SUTTA MINIMALISM (the small map)
What the early suttas actually pin down: world = the six internal/external
sense fields and their contact-chain. [READ — SN 35.107 Lokasamudaya,
FoJin tid 1408, banked]: "Lokassa, bhikkhave, samudayañca atthaṅgamañca
desessāmi… Cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca uppajjati cakkhuviññāṇaṃ" — "I will
teach the origin and disappearance of the world: eye and forms and
eye-consciousness arise… contact → feeling → craving → grasping → becoming
→ birth → aging-and-death." The world's origin IS the origin of contact.
No Sumeru, no cosmography required for liberation. The Buddha as world-
minimalist: the map is optional, the chain is not.

### CH.3 — THE 31 PLANES (the big map)
Abhidhamma/Visuddhimagga expansion: kāma/rūpa/arūpa, 11+16+4 = 31, every
plane a psychophysical address produced by kamma. [ACQUIRE: Vism chapter
extracts from in-house PDF — BPS Ñāṇamoli trans.]. The engine paragraph:
[READ — AN 10.216 Saṁsappanīya, FoJin tid 3713, banked]: "Kammassakā,
bhikkhave, sattā kammadāyādā kammayonī kammabandhū kammapaṭisaraṇā" —
beings are owners, heirs, WOMB, kindred and refuge of their actions; action
distinguishes them as inferior and superior. The cosmology has no creator
because it has a different engine: your deeds are the parents.

### CH.4 — THE GOD WHO WAS MADE BY MIND (the trapdoor)
[READ — DN 1 Brahmajāla, FoJin urn sc/dn1.1, banked Pali]: the first being
in the empty brahma-world, "manomayo pītibhakkho" (mind-made, fed on
rapture), alone for an aeon, concludes: "ahamasmi brahmā mahābrahmā …
kattā nimmātā … pitā bhūtabhabyānaṃ" — "I am Brahmā, the maker, the
creator, father of all that are and are to be. These beings were made by
me." Then beings are reborn there — and THEY believe him. The punchline
[banked]: "Iti mama ca manopaṇidhi, ime ca sattā itthattaṃ āgatā" — "by
MY MIND-PREFERENCE these beings came." The creator-god's own origin story
is mind-made + survivor bias. Canon built the theodicy trap into its own
opening fable. [READ — MN 99 Subho, FoJin urn sc/mn99.1]: the Buddha tells
the brahmin the "path to Brahmā's company" — it's the brahmavihāras.
Cosmology as practice-address: the heaven is reachable by method.

### CH.5 — THE WHEEL: SIX REALMS (the famous map)
The popular map: deva/asura/human/animal/hungry-ghost/hell — as karmic
weather-system. [READ — DN 26 Cakkavatti, FoJin urn sc/dn26.1, banked:
the decline to a ten-year lifespan ("girls marriageable at five"), food
flavors vanishing, weapons turning to grass] — TIME-cosmology: the world
doesn't just have layers, it DETERIORATES on a schedule, then recovers.
Shared-worldview paragraph: every school keeps the wheel; they disagree on
where the hub is (Abhidhamma = tables, Tibetan = poisons, Pure Land =
exit-ramp). [in-house: tibetan-book-of-dead-reference.md six-bardo table ·
three-pure-land-sutras.pdf · Vism PDF].

### CH.6 — THE PURE LAND: COSMOLOGY AS PROMISE (the exit)
The Pure Land is the odd member: a geographically specific cosmology
(Amitābha's Sukhāvatī, 48 vows) that functions as ONE-STEP transport — the
only school where the cosmology IS the soteriology: the map doesn't
describe where you are, it's a promise about where you're going. [ACQUIRE:
exact lines from the in-house three-sutras PDF — Larger Sutra vow lines].

### CH.7 — ARE THEY MENTAL? (the crux — owner's question)
Three readings, all primary, none invented:
- **Realist-kammic** (CH.3): realms are produced like everything else —
  dependently, kammically, without a maker. [AN 10.216 again]
- **Mind-made** (CH.4): Dhp 1–2 [READ — FoJin urn sc/dhp1-20.1, banked]:
  "Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā" — "Phenomena are preceded
  by mind, ruled by mind, MADE OF MIND." The most-quoted line in every
  psychology-of-religion anthology — sitting two books from the 31-plane
  manuals. [ANALAYO IN HOUSE: analayo-rebirth.txt line ~9818 context].
  Plus MN 9 / SN 35.107: even "the world" as the Buddha uses the word is
  the sense-field field. The strong mental reading has primary feet.
- **Deconstructed** (the high-canonical cut) [READ — Kangyur Toh 174,
  FoJin urn 84k/174.1, banked]: "The wise understand the desire realm, the
  form realm, and the formless realm to be utterly nonexistent realms …
  The absence of realms is indicated and articulated by using realms …
  the wise thus understand the three realms to be nonrealms." — realms are
  VOCABULARY with no inherent existence: "shown" the way you'd show
  emptiness with a finger. And [READ — Dhp 167 range, urn sc/dhp167-178.1]:
  "Look upon the world as a bubble or a mirage."
Then the synthesis the sources actually force: mental ≠ unreal-for-you
(kamma still lands — CH.3), real ≠ solid (Toh174 + Dhp bubble), so the
question "are they all mental?" answers YES in register (mind-made,
mind-riddled, empty-of-self) and NO in address (the deed-womb produces
births, not moods). [GUARD: against both New-Age flattening ("all just
psychology" — then AN 10.216's birth-language loses its meaning) and
naive literalism ("solid heavens" — then the Buddha's own minimalism and
Toh174's nonrealms are ignored).]

### CH.8 — THE INSTRUMENT: NOT A MAP OF THE SOLAR SYSTEM (the unsaid)
> Owner thesis (2026-10-21): "are these literally maps of the cosmos
> basically where awareness is the ground truth and instead of navigating
> external planes you are going internal and the body is the doorway to the
> universe… it's not like a map of the solar system… science is basically
> exploring the external world with sensors yet they will always find
> something that looks like information because thats the most granular you
> can get its the minimal unit, in order to go beyond that you need a finer
> sensor which is consciousness itself." — treated here as the film's
> interpretive key; every leg below carries a fetched source.

**Beat 1 — the tradition says the map is made of knowledge, not survey.**
The Buddha's title isn't "world-cartographer" — it's lokavidū, KNOWER of
the world. And the construction method is stated in the formula [READ —
DN 4, FoJin tid 233, banked Pali]: "So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ
sabrahmakaṁ … sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti" — "this world with its
gods, its Māras, its Brahmās … having realized it with his OWN KNOWLEDGE,
he declares it." The instrument is named too [READ — Toh 10, FoJin tid
5164, banked]: "With their purified divine eye that far surpasses the
human, they see beings that are dying and being born … exactly in accord
with the action." Survey needs roads; this needs a finer sensor — and the
canon says so in its own equipment list. Local material for the visual
beat [Vism XII, in-house PDF]: the Buddha "split the earth in two and
showed Avīci, and he parted the sky in two and showed the Brahmā-world" —
and the elder Dhammadinna "turned his fan face upwards and an opening
right up to the Brahmā-world appeared." The map is OPENED FROM HERE, not
traveled to.

**Beat 2 — body as doorway: the measured universe IS the measured body
(Kālacakra, the most literal version anyone wrote).** [SCHOLARSHIP —
Berzin, StudyBuddhism, fetched]: Kālacakra maps every measurement onto the
human frame — "From the bottom of the wind mandala to the peak of Mount
Meru is 400,000 yojanas. This parallels the measurement from the bottom of
the feet to the top of the head being four cubits… Mount Meru being
100,000 yojanas parallels … the spine being one cubit. The neck of Mount
Meru … parallels the neck of the human body." Not metaphor-by-mood: the
same numbers. And the stated ground beneath BOTH maps [SCHOLARSHIP —
Berzin via Shambhala, fetched]: "A description of the external and internal
worlds in terms of their unifying parallels reveals the shared underlying
basis from which both derive, namely, clear light mind. The winds of karma
that provide the impulses for a particular universe to evolve come from
the collective karma on the clear light minds of prior beings." — the
tradition's own "awareness is the ground truth" sentence. Sutta-side
version already banked: the brahma-world is reached by a METHOD — mettā
[B5, MN 99]: the address was never spatial, it was always a state you can
enter. "The body is the doorway" is not a modern gloss; Kālacakra says
the universe and the body have literally the same measures and the same
basis (Berzin: "the proportions and shape of the universe, human body,
and the mandala … are all the same" — fetched).

**Beat 2b — is Meru a physical place? No — it works like a deity body
(owner's reading, 2026-10-21).** Two fetched/in-house anchors make this
textual, not New-Age: (i) [SCHOLARSHIP — Samye, fetched]: "Mount Meru …
a sacred mountain in Indian cosmology that does not correspond to any
known geophysical location"; and the map-never-was-navigation line (S4).
(ii) The TANTRA PARALLEL, where the owner's instinct is exact: in
generation stage the deity body is not drawn, it is BUILT — deities are
mind-made in the same precise sense as DN 1's `manomayo` [B3]; and the
system says so about itself [in-house: tibetan-book-of-dead-reference.md,
fetched this session from library/]: Guhyagarbha Tantra — "first work to
portray the 42 peaceful and 58 wrathful deities as the purified forms of
ordinary psycho-physical states." Deities = states, displayed as bodies;
Meru = same measurements as the body, because cosmos/body/mandala are
one object at three scales (S4). So the tantric answer to "where is
Meru": the same place your generation-stage body is — produced by
practice, real in the register the manual operates. [ACQUIRE: Dyczkowski
`journey-in-the-world-of-the-tantras` (in-house PDF) for the one
deity-body line in a tantric scholar's own words; note SERIES-GLOBAL's
TĀ 10.292–307 material (consciousness relocalizing, not traveling) as
the Śaiva cousin of this same argument — cross-wing only, no callback].

**Beat 3 — the science mirror: every sensor bottoms out at information.**
[SCIENCE, banked — Wheeler, "Information, physics, quantum: the search
for links," 1989/1990, fetched from the paper PDF]: "No element in the
description of physics shows itself as closer to primordial than the
elementary quantum phenomenon, that is, the elementary device-
intermediated act of posing a yes-no physical question and eliciting an
answer … Otherwise stated, every physical quantity, every it, derives its
ultimate significance from bits, binary yes-or-no indications, a
conclusion which we epitomize in the phrase, it from bit." Science, pushed
to its own floor, arrives at: the most granular thing is an ANSWER —
information — and answers require an asking. The instrument is inside the
result. That is precisely where the owner's argument lands: to go past the
information floor you don't need a bigger sensor, you need a finer one —
and every contemplative tradition claims to be running one.

**Beat 4 — the synthesis AND the difference (the honest cut).**
Both systems hit the same wall — the measuring is prior to the measured —
and they part ways on what it means: Wheeler stays physicalist
("information-theoretic in origin … a participatory universe"), the canon
makes the ground not information but liberation-from-the-whole-sensorium
(SN 35.107 [B7]: even "the world" is the six fields and their chain). So
the verdict on "are these maps of the cosmos?": NOT in the solar-system
sense — [SCHOLARSHIP — Berzin, fetched] "neither was formulated for the
sake of navigation." They are maps of where consciousness lands, what it
builds, and what opens when the instrument is refined — WHICH IS WHY the
same tradition can hold 31 planes, nonrealms, and body-measures without
flinching: three readings of one instrument manual. [GUARD: two prices
paid out loud — (a) mysticism-flattening ("it's all just the mind") is
refuted by B1: the deed-womb produces BIRTHS, not moods; (b) science-
proves-Buddhism cheap shots are refuted by the parting: the canon claims
cessation, Wheeler claims participation, and that difference is the
film's last cut — not waved away.]

### CH.9 — THE SHARED WORLDVIEW (the agreement table)
Internal agreement table (structure seed — expand into table): (1) engine =
kamma, never creator (all schools) · (2) address = dependent-arising, not
substance (Madhyamaka makes this explicit, Abhidhamma implies it in tables)
· (3) hub = the human realm (only plane that can practice; all maps agree)
· (4) time = deteriorating cycles (DN 26 + kaliyuga-adjacent echoes) ·
(5) the exit disagreements: map-study vs poison-work vs vow-transport vs
seeing-through. What they share even when they contradict: NOBODY IS IN
CHARGE.

### CH.10 — GUARD + CLOSE
Guard: one more time — the map is not the claim; the chain is. This film's
own close-line: "the realm you're in is the one your actions are building."
Close: the agreement table (CH.9) holds — five maps, one engine, nobody in
charge — so the question was never "which cosmology is right" but "what
kind of thing are you assuming a realm is before you ask." [DIR: table →
wheel → blank white.]
