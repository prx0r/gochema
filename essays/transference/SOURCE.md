## KARMA CHAKME — load-bearing extracts (CC BY-NC, attributed)

**Six types:** "dharmakāya transference, sambhogakāya transference, nirmāṇakāya
transference, ordinary transference, forceful transference, and the transference
of entering another body."

**Dharmakāya (the honest-boundary line):** "the ultimate form of transference,
free from the concepts of something to be transferred or one who transfers."

**Forceful transference condemned:** "To transfer at the wrong time is to kill
the deities… it would bring about the transgression of killing deities and the
transgression of suicide… even worse than the five deeds of immediate
fruition." (Three deceiving-death rituals must come first.)

**Entering another body — the lineage is DEAD:** "It is well known that the
right interdependent circumstances did not come together for this teaching to
be passed on in Tibet. And that is why, although the textual transmission
exists, there is no lineage of actual practice." (Skt: purapraveśa — 'entering
the city', city = another's body.) → This is the enter-another-body special's
opening card: the tradition itself says this one is lost.

**Nine doors (exit → rebirth):** superior — crown→pure lands, eyes→cakravartin,
left nostril→clean human; middling — right nostril→yakṣa, ears→form realm,
navel→desire gods; inferior — urethra→animal, secret place→hungry ghost,
anus→hells.

**Transference for others:** outer breath ceased + vital channel cut → visualize
+ recite; hair pulls free at crown as the sign; window is 3 nights up to 7 days.
