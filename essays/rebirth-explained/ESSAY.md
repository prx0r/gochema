[READ — Visuddhimagga]: "There are three kinds of death: death as cutting
off, momentary death, and conventional death. Death as cutting off belongs

## CH.4 — TOUR 1: THERAVĀDA (7:30–11:00)

what rebirth is in theravada: death is seventeen thought-moments long. five
weak flashes grab the death-object — a past deed, its image, or a sign of
the destination — death-consciousness falls, and in the very next instant,
rebirth-consciousness rises on the same object. no gap. no traveller. your
final mental posture registers a direction and the series is already turning
over.

[READ — Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha §12]: "a rebirth consciousness arises and is
established in the subsequent existence, based on the object thus obtained…
so called because it links together the two consecutive existences."

and the footnote that ruins every pop-rebirth story you've ever heard:
[READ — §13 fn]: "There is a misconception… that the subsequent birth is
conditioned by this last decease-thought. What actually conditions
rebirth… is that which is experienced during the javana process."

your deathbed epiphany is downstream of your actual conduct. and
conception needs three things: [READ — MN 38]: "the descent of the embryo
occurs with the union of three things… mother & father… her season… a
gandhabba is present." miss any one, no birth.

theravada claims the earliest layer of text — their words, their timeline.
the thai forest monks under ajahn lee go further: their teachers checked
their past lives like checking the football scores. that's their claim
anyway. observer here.

[READ — Thanissaro, Truth of Rebirth]: "Prior to awakening, you can't know
these things for sure, but as the Buddha states… it's wisest to assume
these principles as working hypotheses."

## CH.5 — TOUR 2: YOGĀCĀRA (11:00–14:00)

yogacara: no soul, but there's a store. every intentional thing you do drops
a seed into the storehouse consciousness, perfuming it like sesame in oil.
at death the store withdraws from the senses — the body cooling is their
clock — and the seed-account carries over. a ripe seed sprouts as your next
life. you're not a person. you're an accounting entry with better handwriting.

why they built it — the problem that forced the layer: [READ — Waldron,
Ālaya Treatise]: "the subliminal ālayavijñāna, which abides or hides in the
sense faculties during meditation, functions even when all other conscious
states cease to operate." consciousness stops completely in deep absorption,
their own texts say so — something has to be under the floor. and the loop:
[READ]: "Ālayavijñāna functions as the condition for active cognitive
awareness in two ways: by being their seed, and by providing their support" —
while manifest awareness "nurtur[es] the seeds in this life… and caus[es]
ālayavijñāna to persist on into the next life."

the store feeds the moments, the moments feed the store, and death is just
the account moving offices.

and keep this receipt for later: [READ — Asaṅga, Mahāyānasaṃgraha]: the ālaya
is "the same idea which is called root-consciousness (mula-vijñana) by the
Mahasamghika schools and what the Sthavira schools call the bhavaṅga."
one concept, three school names.
