## CH.4 — TOUR 1: THERAVĀDA (7:30–11:00)

### [7:30] nutshell
what rebirth is in theravada: death is seventeen thought-moments long. five
weak flashes grab the death-object — a past deed, its image, or a sign of
the destination — death-consciousness falls, and in the very next instant,
rebirth-consciousness rises on the same object. no gap. no traveller. no
luggage carousel. what's going on: your final mental posture registers a
direction and the series is already turning over. you don't go anywhere.
the queue does.

### [8:30] the mechanism, their words
[READ — Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha §12]: "a rebirth consciousness arises and is
established in the subsequent existence, based on the object thus obtained…
so called because it links together the two consecutive existences."

### [9:15] the footnote everyone misses
and here's the footnote that ruins every pop-rebirth story you've ever
heard: [READ — §13 fn]: "There is a misconception… that the subsequent birth
is conditioned by this last decease-thought. What actually conditions
rebirth… is that which is experienced during the javana process." the famous
last thought doesn't decide. the five flashes before it do. your deathbed
epiphany is downstream of your actual conduct.

### [10:00] conception needs three things
[READ — MN 38]: "the descent of the embryo occurs with the union of three
things… mother & father… her season… a gandhabba is present."

three requirements, all mechanical, zero theology. miss any one, no birth.
and for the "who's watching" crowd: they're careful with the word 'being' —
but we'll get to that when we get to the school that specialises in careful.

### [10:40] says who + forest card

## CH.5 — TOUR 2: YOGĀCĀRA (11:00–14:00)

### [11:00] nutshell
yogacara takes one look at that and says: cute, but where's the storage.
no soul — fine — but every intentional thing you do drops a seed into the
storehouse consciousness, perfuming it like sesame in oil. at death the
store withdraws from the senses — the body cooling is their clock — and the
seed-account carries over. a ripe seed sprouts as your next life. which is
why your next life rhymes with this one's habits. you're not a person.
you're an accounting entry with better handwriting.

### [11:50] why they built it
here's the story that makes the whole school make sense. they had a real
problem: if consciousness stops completely in deep meditation — and their
own texts say it does — what restarts it? nothing preceding it. so they
built a layer that runs beneath the floor. the ālaya. [READ — Ālaya Treatise
via Waldron]: "Ālayavijñāna functions as the condition for active cognitive
awareness in two ways: by being their seed, and by providing their support" —
and manifest awareness feeds back: it "nurtur[es] the seeds in this life…
and caus[es] ālayavijñāna to persist on into the next life."

that loop is the whole machine: the store feeds the moments, the moments
feed the store, and the moment of death is just the account moving offices.
