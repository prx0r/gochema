## THE GLOBAL SHARED ONTOLOGY (the film's twist)
v1's table said "no soul travels — everyone agrees." Globally the answer
splits into four carrier-families:
- **Something persists as self**: Hindu ātman, Greek soul, Jewish neshamah
  (where affirmed), Daoist spirit-body.
- **Something persists without self**: Buddhist continuum/seeds, Jain pudgala.
- **Nobody persists — God re-creates**: Christian resurrection (strictly,
  continuity is *guaranteed by promise*, not mechanism).
- **The relation persists**: Śaiva recognition, Pure Land vow.
One line for the film: "everyone asks the same question; four incompatible
answers; Buddhism was the only one where nobody's home — which is either
the flaw or the whole point."

## Visual twist
Same pipeline (fluid body + transfer animations), but each tradition gets a
**body-skin**: Egyptian = ka-arms + heart-scale overlay; Greek = urn/silhouette
geometry; Christian = the SAME body twice (resurrection = re-materialization,
anti-transfer!); Śaiva = deitybody waves (finally its native home); Hindu =
clay-shirt metaphor (Gītā 2.22: a person putting off old clothes) as the
literal animation. The Christian stop is the visual joke of the film: NOTHING
transfers, the body just… comes back.
