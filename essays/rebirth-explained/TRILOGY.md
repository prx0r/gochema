## The arc
1. **Buddhist Reincarnation, Actually Explained** — the schools map
   (v1, schools only). ENDS ON THE CLIFFHANGER: after the seven nutshells
   and the shared ontology, the final beat is the Nanavira/Nanamanda
   psychological interpretation — rebirth is *constantly happening*, the
   vortex re-forming moment to moment — then a comment on Buddhist notions
   of time (kṣaṇa/santāna; the past entering the present like Bergson's
   melody, held as tension not explained). Cut to black on the unresolved
   feeling. ENDING WORDS ARE OWNER'S (placeholder S16, owner writes).
2. **Stream Entry Actually Explained** — the deep dive: Nanavira +
   Nanamanda dependent origination, human experience derived from the
   ground up. Beats: habits → karma → the vortex → self (subject/object
   co-arising) → sotāpatti as recognition (Nanavira's 27.6.1959 Eye of
   Dhamma) → what stream-entry *is* (removal of the error, not addition).
   The fire-simile material (already in production) lives here as a spine
   section, not as its own film.
3. **Nirvana Actually Explained** — the payoff: cessation, the fire
   sermon's answer, unborn/unbecome (Ud 8:3), what "extinguished" means,
   the forest sunbeam image + the Madhyamaka dissolution as double ending.
