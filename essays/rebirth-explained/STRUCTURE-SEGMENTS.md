### The exact-moment definitions (compiled — sources in repo)
- **Theravāda — THREE deaths** (Visuddhimagga, Dhammapala legend, verbatim
  in repo): "There are three kinds of death: death as cutting off, momentary
  death, and conventional death. Death as cutting off belongs to those whose
  cankers are exhausted. Momentary death is that of each consciousness of the
  cognitive series… Conventional death is that of all (so-called) living beings."
  → Operational moment: cuti-citta — bhavanga vibrates, 5 weak javanas take
  the death-object, cuti falls, paṭisandhi next instant (AbSanga §11–13).
- **Tibetan — the dissolution finishes:** earth→water→fire→wind (last breath
  wheezes), red → white → black (swoon, 80 impressions cease) → GROUND
  LUMINOSITY dawns. Death = end of dissolution + clarity (reference table,
  library/tibetan, ch.11 of the Bardo Thödol).
- **Yogācāra — the store checks out:** ālaya "keeps the body alive… only
  withdrawing from it (with the body becoming cold) at the time of death"
  (SEP) — moment = withdrawal from sense faculties, body cools.
- **Pure Land — the deathbed:** "on their deathbeds, see him and a host of
  sages welcoming them" (BDK Large Sutra) — moment = last thought oriented
  to the Name; the welcome replaces the mechanics.
- **Madhyamaka — refuses the question:** only conventional designation
  ("breakup of the body"); no svabhāva of a death-moment to find (Yamaka/
  Anurādha). The definition *is* the refusal.
- **Nāṇavīra/HH — refuses the EVENT:** "our birth is always here with us…
  his birth is present" — time secondary; the "exact moment" reading is the
  category error (With birth, death applies). Rhyme to bank: Vism's
  *momentary death* and HH's *present birth* converge — the two most
  precise texts in the set say death/birth are happening per-instant.

### Primacy claims (each school says it's the true one)
- Theravāda: earliest textual layer, closest to the Buddha (Analayo).
- Yogācāra: fixes Abhidharma's broken continuity problem (the store that
  integrates momentariness — Waldron).
- Pure Land: the only one that works in the degenerate age (Easy Path).
- Madhyamaka: the airtight one — nothing left to attack.
- Tibetan: the full experiential map — and tukdam is its data point.
- Nāṇavīra/HH: reads the Canon as written — jāti in p.s. means nature, not
  past event; no cosmology needed.
