## 3. NDE SCIENCE — the honest middle (S15 hard-questions slot)
- **Parnia et al. 2023, AWARE-II**, Resuscitation — 567 in-hospital cardiac
  arrests; 11/28 interviewed survivors reported CA memories; 6/28 (21.4%)
  transcendent Recalled Experience of Death; **normal EEG (delta/theta/alpha)
  re-emerged 35–60 min into CPR** despite mean rSO₂ 43%. Verbatim in review.
- **Mashour, Lee, Pal, Li 2024**, PMC — "Consciousness and the Dying Brain":
  dying-brain gamma surges (rodent + human), BUT "no evidence to correlate
  the neurophysiologic changes with any conscious experience." The
  counterweight — cite both sides, same card.
- **Borjigin et al. 2013**, PNAS — surge of neurophysiological coherence and
  connectivity in the dying brain (rats). **Xu et al. 2023**, PNAS — gamma
  surge in dying human brains.
- Note: owner's film skips NDE-lit as *evidence* (per draft1) — these live in
  the v2 hard-questions video as the "what science actually shows" card.

## 4. BUDDHIST METAPHYSICS × CURRENT SCIENCE (shared-ontology beat)
- **Boly et al. 2024** (bioRxiv, "Neural correlates of pure presence") —
  Vajrayana + Zen meditators reach "pure presence" (luminosity without
  objects/self); hdEEG shows **gamma DECREASE** + delta also low — cortex
  "highly awake" with reduced activity. Explicitly tests **IIT prediction**.
  This is the Luminosity/card from the Tibetan nutshell measured. CLIP: Boly
  on the Tukdam Project talk.

  predictive-processing models. The bridge sentence science ↔ Abhidhamma,
  published.
- **Thompson 2015** — dreamless-sleep debate (Advaita vs Nyāya) as challenge
