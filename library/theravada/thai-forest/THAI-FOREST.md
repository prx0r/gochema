> Owner: "we are also missing thai buddhism school here right? ajahn lee" — correct.

## What the school is
Forest tradition of Ajahn Sao → Ajahn Mun → Ajahn Lee Dhammadharo → Ajahn Chah
→ western lineages (Thanissaro at Metta Forest etc.). Dhammayut + Maha Nikaya
kammatthāna. Inherits standard Theravāda Abhidhamma rebirth mechanics
(paṭisandhi) but adds three operational layers:

1. **Past-life recollection as pedagogy** — pubbenivāsānussati checked
   routinely by the masters (Ajahn Mun's biography culture; "the teacher
   saw your last three lifetimes" is normal forest-talk). Verification beat
   in the film: the school where rebirth is *tested*, not believed.
2. **Adhiṭṭhāna** — determination-power persisting past death (Mun's
   "adhiṭṭhāna elephant", teachings remaining accessible to disciples).
   Rebirth-adjacent phenomenon, Thai-specific. Hard-question card.
3. **Psychic powers (iddhi)** treated as real working tools (Lee's manuals
   discuss them practically), not folk residue.
