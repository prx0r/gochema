# READING NOTES — Thanissaro, *The Truth of Rebirth* (read in full 2026-10-19)

> 8 chapters, 72pp, CC BY-NC-SA, Metta Forest. PDF + full text in this folder.
> Purpose in gochema: the v2 "why does it matter" answer bank + the honest
> Theravāda position the film quotes verbatim.

## The wager frame (ch.0)
Every action is a wager on consequences; "I don't know" is itself a wager
(that it won't matter). Apaṇṇaka: the safe-bet teaching. Pre-awakening you
can't *know* rebirth — so the Buddha offers working hypotheses + a path that
confirms them.

## Ch.1 — the reversal (the book's core polemic)
The West's "drop the worldview, keep the psychology" move = 1800s liberal
Christianity replayed on Buddhism. Thanissaro's five reversals:
1. Rebirth was NOT a universal Indian assumption (annihilationists existed).
2. The Buddha entered a hot debate — chose to address it because conducive
   to suffering's end (unlike cosmic-eternity questions, MN 63).
3. Rebirth is structurally integral: 4NT lists birth first; dependent
   co-arising is self-sustaining feedback loops — every factor has an
   implicit "re-" from re-ignorance to re-death. Without assuming repeated
   birth, he'd never have probed BEYOND birth to its causes (SN 12:65).
4. His treatment is phenomenological: refuses to say WHAT is reborn
   (SN 12:35, SN 12:12 "not a valid question"; SN 44:10 declines the
   self-existence question). DO doesn't occur *in* a metaphysical context —
   DO is the context in which selves/worlds are seen as constructions.
5. Same test applies to moderns: put your identity-metaphysics down.

## Ch.2 — the ancient controversy (film gold)
Ajita Kesakambala's materialism (DN 2): "generosity is taught by idiots… with
breakup of body, wise and foolish alike are annihilated." Prince Pāyāsi's
quasi-experiments (DN 23): live men sealed in clay jars in furnaces — "maybe
we'll see his soul escaping" — and strangling-and-weighing: the dead are
heavier. Seven annihilationist types; four eternalist; pro-rebirth schools
disagree among themselves (Makkhali: determinism — ball of string unwinds;
Pakudha: seven unalterable substances). Kalāma four assurances (AN 3:66) —
proof the question was live.

## Ch.3 — rebirth & action
Three knowledges of the Buddha's awakening (MN 19) all involve rebirth.
Mundane right view (MN 117) = verbatim negation of Ajita. Why karma needs
lifetimes: MN 36/136 — the immoral man rewarded now and punished after;
SN 42:13 — the king's killer wears garlands, the murderer walks to the
drum; "all misconduct suffers here-and-now" is flatly false, so multi-life
perspective is required for the moral case to hold. Safe-bet double-throw
(MN 60). Faith defined: admission of ignorance + willingness to adopt
assumptions for a path that seems likely to work (MN 27).

## Ch.4 — the noble truth of rebirth
Saṁvega fuel: tears-ocean verse (SN 15:3). Birth first in the 1NT list;
2NT = "craving that makes for further becoming." Scale-constancy thesis:
the PROCESS is the constant (like c in relativity); DO is presented
scale-free — erosion-pattern photo without a tree for scale. Micro=macro
is the Buddha's third-knowledge move.

## Ch.5 — appropriate frame (the film's hardest-question answer)
"Which is the birth, and whose is the birth?" → NOT A VALID QUESTION (SN
12:35). Consciousness-nutriment "for what?" not "who feeds?" (SN 12:12).
Sutta 23:2: a "being" = being caught up in craving for any aggregate —
so a being can be born/die many times a day. The antidote to metaphysical
entanglement is appropriate attention to the 4NT as experienced.

## Ch.6 — food for rebirth (the mechanism chapter)
Upādāna = clinging AND fuel. SN 44.9 flame (our central quote, in context).
MN 38 gandhabba three-conditions. Four nutriments; craving produces the
food it feeds on (MN 38). DN 15 consciousness↔name-form mutual causality;
Sāriputta's two sheaves of reeds (SN 12:67 — Nanavira's reeds live here).
AN 3:77: karma = field, consciousness = seed, craving = moisture. Strategy:
STARVE, don't destroy — destroying identity just continues becoming.

## Ch.7 — choosing dispassion
Dust-and-earth rebirth odds (SN 56:102–113). 300 spears/day for 100 years
in exchange for the 4NT — take the deal (SN 56:35). Darkness greater than
intergalactic void (SN 56:46). Jhāna as the alternate pleasure that frees
from sensuality (MN 14) but itself a becoming to be contemplated as
aggregates (AN 9:36 archer; MN 106 equanimity-mistaken-for-nibbana trap).
Landing-light: SN 12:64 sunbeam that lands nowhere without a western wall.
Ending: "Unprovoked is my release. This is the last birth" (SN 56:11);
consciousness-without-surface (MN 49); Ud 8:3 unborn.

## Ch.8 — modern ironies
Three materialist objections + rebuttals: (a) "beyond range" = beyond
*description*, not knowledge (AN 4:173 Mahākotthita-Sāriputta four
positions; SN 35:117 "that dimension should be experienced"); (b) can't
practice without rebirth — ignores appropriate attention + self-sustaining
loops; (c) modern awakening without rebirth-knowledge self-refutes (the
canonical marks include ending birth; mismatched label = examine the label).
Plus: science can't prove free will it presupposes; the psychology/cosmology
separation is itself an unexamined cultural assumption.
