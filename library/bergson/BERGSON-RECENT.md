## The direct hits (Bergson ↔ Buddhist)
1. **Sudeep Raj Kumar 2024**, Sophia (Springer, Aug 2024): "Buddhaghosa's
   Model of Temporality seen through the Prism of Bergson's Duration."
   Our exact combo — Visuddhimagga temporality × durée. In-house cite:
   we hold the Visuddhimagga (853pp) + Bergson is banked in essay1.
2. **WooHyun Jung 2026**, Philosophy East and West (Oct 2026 — THIS
   MONTH): "The Kṣaṇa and the Living Present: Husserl's Time-Consciousness
   and Buddhist Momentariness." Momentariness ≠ pointillism; the santāna
   carries continuity — the Abhidhamma side of the 17-moment strip.
3. **Anawat Bunnag 2017/18** (SSRN, Mahidol): "Buddhist Causality and
   Bergson's Duration: A Defense from Theravāda Viewpoint" — duration
   doesn't reject causality, it ENHANCES the causal chain (karma beat).
4. **Yaobinbin 2014** (ECNU, Chinese Studies): the 1921 Bergsonism–Buddhism
   debate — Zhang Taiyan & Liang Shuming argued intuitionism/élan ≈
   ālayavijñāna/vijñānavāda; Lü Cheng & Liang opposed. The alaya↔Bergson
   map is a century old, not ours.
5. **B.V.E. Hyde 2021/23** (Durham, SSRN): Bergsonism as extreme dualism,
   BUT "remarkable how Buddhist his metaphysic is" — critic's concession.
6. **Mohit Abrol 2023**, Bergsoniana: Bergson × Nishida Kitarō self/world
   mapping, Zen + Theravāda citations — the East-Asian bridge.
